Leith Bass Space, Harmonic Elements: the recorded sounds
========================================================

Every recording in these packs is in the public domain (Creative Commons
Zero, CC0 1.0). No credit is required. These are the people who made and gave
them away:

kit-studio.mp3
    Virtuosity Drums. Versilian Studios, recorded at Virtuosity Musical
    Instruments, Boston, played by Austin McMahon.
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums

kit-rock.mp3, kit-brushes.mp3
    Big Rusty Drums. Karoryfer Samples.
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums

guitar.mp3
    Black And Green Guitars (the green Gretsch Anniversary). Karoryfer
    Samples, recorded by Brian Wood.
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars

grand.mp3 (Steinway B), upright.mp3 (Knight), vibes.mp3, and the claps and
tambourines in perc.mp3
    VCSL, the Versilian Community Sample Library. Versilian Studios and
    contributors (the Knight upright was sampled by Simon Dalzell, Ivy Audio).
    https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

The shaker, congas, claves and cowbell in perc.mp3
    The auxiliary percussion of Virtuosity Drums, from VSCO 2. Versilian
    Studios.

ebass.mp3
    Black And Blue Basses: the black one, a Washburn AB-95 five string
    played with the fingers on its neck pickup, recorded direct.
    Karoryfer Samples.
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses

ubass.mp3
    Meatbass (a 1958 double bass, plucked). Karoryfer Samples.
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.meatbass

clicks.mp3 (the Shed metronome's clicks)
    The woodblocks, claves and cowbells are from VCSL (Versilian Studios
    and contributors); the cross stick and the hi hat are from Virtuosity
    Drums.

Each pack was cut down to the notes and hits the app uses, mixed to one
channel and saved as MP3 by build_packs.py. Everything else the app plays
(electric pianos, organ, strings, pads, brass, nylon guitar, the 808 and the
909) is made in the browser and uses no recordings at all.
